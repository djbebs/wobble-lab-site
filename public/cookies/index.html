Cookies set by this site

None. The site stores no cookie of its own and keeps no identifier for you.

Cookies set by advertising

Advertising is supplied by Google. Depending on your choice in the consent banner, Google and its partners may store cookies or read similar identifiers in your browser to select advertisements, limit how often you see the same one, and measure performance.

If you decline personalised advertising, advertisements may still be shown, selected without the use of such identifiers.

Changing your choice

The consent banner can be reopened at any time from this page once advertising is live. Your previous answer is remembered until you change it or clear your browser storage.

Local storage

The simulations write nothing to local storage. Choices you make inside an experiment, such as a colour or a name for the specimen, live only in the page and are gone when you reload. If a future experiment saves a setting for your convenience, it will be listed here first.
